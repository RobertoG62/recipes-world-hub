Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This is a static content site. It does not collect personal information about you: no forms, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising pixels. Every file loads from this site's own server - there are no third-party requests.

Who runs this site

World Recipes Hub is a personal content project by Roberto Goldreer. There is no commerce, no sales and no lead collection.

Privacy contact: robertog1692@gmail.com

What this site does not collect

  • •No forms, no input fields and no registration.
  • •No user accounts and no passwords.
  • •No cookies of any kind.
  • •No Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, no other analytics tool.
  • •No advertising networks, and no sale or sharing of data with third parties.

What does happen - stated plainly

Language preference. When you switch between English and Hebrew, the choice is saved in your browser's localStorage under the key wrh-lang. It holds one value: 'en' or 'he'. It never leaves your browser, it is not personal information, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Hosting logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub records requests - including your IP address, browser type and time of request - in order to serve the site, secure it and prevent abuse. That logging is done by GitHub, not by us, and we have no access to those logs.

No third-party resources. Fonts, stylesheets, icons and scripts are all stored on this site and are not loaded from services such as Google Fonts or a CDN. That means simply browsing here does not expose your IP address to any company beyond the host.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is no record for us to retrieve, correct or delete. If you still have a question or request about privacy, write to the address above and we will respond.

Changes

If a form, analytics tool or any external component is ever added, this policy will be updated before it goes live, and the date at the top will change.
